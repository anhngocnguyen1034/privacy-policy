NewTrust built the Camera Translator app as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by NewTrust at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone who uses this Service. If you choose to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.

What the App Does On Your Device

Translation, text recognition from images, and language detection run on your device using Google ML Kit. Language models are downloaded once and then work offline. The photos you translate, the text recognised in them, and the screen images captured for on-screen translation are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us and not stored on any server we operate.

Your translation history, saved bookmarks, and dictionary lookup history are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We have no copy of them.

Permissions We Request, and Why

  • Camera — to show the live preview and take the photo you want translated. Used only while a camera screen is open.
  • Microphone — to capture speech for voice translation and conversation mode. Used only while you are actively recording. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service built into your Android device, which may send the audio to its provider (usually Google) for processing under that provider's own privacy policy.
  • Screen capture — for on-screen translation. Android asks for your explicit consent each time, and a notification stays visible for as long as capture is active. Captured frames are read for text and then discarded.
  • Display over other apps — to show the floating translation bubble and translated overlays.
  • Internet and network state — to download language models, look up dictionary entries, and check whether you are online.
  • Notifications — to show the required ongoing notification while screen translation is running.

Information Sent Off Your Device

When you look up a word in the Dictionary, that single word is sent to dictionaryapi.dev to retrieve its definition. No account information, device identifier, or other personal data is attached to that request.

When you contact us using the feedback option in Settings, your message is sent to us by email using your own email app, so we receive whatever address you send it from.

The app has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. It is entirely free.

We also collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase — which features are opened and how often — so we can decide what to improve. These statistics do not include the content of what you translate.
