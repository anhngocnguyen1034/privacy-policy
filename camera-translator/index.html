Privacy Policy

NewTrust built the Camera Translator app as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by NewTrust at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone who uses this Service. If you choose to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. You do not create an account and you do not sign in. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising.

What the App Does On Your Device

Translation, text recognition from images, and language detection run on your device using Google ML Kit. The language and text recognition models are downloaded once from Google's servers through Google Play services, and after that they work offline. The photos and documents you translate, the text recognised in them, and the screen images captured for on-screen translation are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us and not stored on any server we operate.

What Is Stored On Your Device

The following are stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your translation and scan history, including a small thumbnail of a scanned image, kept up to a fixed maximum number of recent entries.
  • Your saved bookmarks and your dictionary lookup history.
  • Your settings, such as the interface language, the chosen translation languages, dark mode, offline mode, and voice options.

You can delete these from inside the app using the clear option on the history and bookmark screens. Uninstalling the app deletes all of them. We have no copy of them.

Permissions We Request, and Why

  • Camera — to show the live preview and take the photo you want translated. Used only while a camera screen is open. Photos taken for translation are not saved to your gallery unless you ask for them to be saved.
  • Microphone — to capture speech for voice translation and conversation mode. Used only while you are actively recording. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service built into your Android device, which may send the audio to its provider (usually Google) for processing under that provider's own privacy policy. We do not record, keep, or transmit the audio ourselves.
  • Screen capture — for on-screen translation. Android asks for your explicit consent each time, and a notification stays visible for as long as capture is active. Captured frames are read for text and then discarded; they are never written to storage or sent anywhere.
  • Display over other apps — to show the floating translation bubble and translated overlays.
  • Internet and network state — to download language models, look up dictionary entries, and check whether you are online.
  • Notifications — to show the required ongoing notification while screen translation is running.
  • Run a foreground service and keep the device awake — so that on-screen translation keeps working while it is switched on and the screen does not sleep in the middle of it.

The app does not request access to your photo library, your files, your contacts, your location, or your phone identifiers.

Photos and Documents You Choose

When you pick an image from your gallery, or open a PDF, DOCX, or PPTX file to translate, you choose the file through the Android system picker and the app receives only that one file. No storage permission is needed and the app cannot browse the rest of your files. The text is extracted and translated on your device, and the file is not uploaded to us.

If you tap save on a translated image, the app writes that image to your gallery in a folder named Translator. If you tap share, the app hands the image or text to the app you choose. Both happen only when you ask.

Information Sent Off Your Device

  • Dictionary lookups. When you look up a word in the Dictionary, that single word is sent to the Wikimedia REST API at en.wiktionary.org to retrieve its definition. No account information, device identifier, or other personal data is attached to that request. Wikimedia's handling of the request is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Speech recognition. Your recorded speech is passed to the speech recognition service on your device, which may send it to its provider (usually Google) to be transcribed.
  • Reading text aloud. When you tap the speaker button, the text is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (usually Google's). That engine may send the text to its provider to synthesise the voice, and may download voice data.
  • Language model downloads. ML Kit downloads translation and text recognition models from Google's servers through Google Play services.
  • Analytics and crash reports. Described in the two sections below.
  • Feedback. When you contact us using the feedback option in Settings, your message is sent to us by email using your own email app, so we receive whatever address you send it from, together with whatever you write.
  • Rating the app. If you choose to rate the app, the rating is handled by Google Play. We receive only what Google Play shows every developer.

Usage Statistics

We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase so we can decide what to improve. These are events such as which screen was opened, which button was tapped, which language pair was selected, whether a translation succeeded, and the length in characters of the text you translated or looked up.

These statistics never include the content of what you translate — not the text, not the recognised words, not the images, not the audio, and not the documents you open. Google attaches to them a per-installation app instance identifier and general technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, and approximate country derived from the IP address. The app instance identifier is reset if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Log Data

If the app crashes or hits an error, we collect diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics. This may include your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, the app's configuration at the time, the time and date, the stack trace of the fault, and other statistics. We use it to fix faults, not to identify you.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google ML Kit
  • The speech recognition and text-to-speech services installed on your device, usually Google's
  • Wikimedia Foundation (Wiktionary definitions)

How Long Data Is Kept, and How to Delete It

History, bookmarks, dictionary history, thumbnails, and settings stay on your device until you clear them in the app or uninstall it. Usage statistics and crash reports are held by Google on our behalf under Firebase's retention settings, which we keep at Google's default period, after which they are deleted or aggregated. If you want us to delete the diagnostic and usage data associated with your installation, write to the address at the end of this policy and tell us when and on what device you used the app, and we will delete what we can identify.
