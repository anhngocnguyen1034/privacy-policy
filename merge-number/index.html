anhnn built Merge Number as a free number puzzle game. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Merge Number has no account and no sign-in, shows no ads and contains no analytics. We do not collect your name, email or any other personal information, and your game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no registration and no user profile, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your scores, best scores and the highest block you have reached.
  • Saved games for Merge Number, 2048 Swipe and Cross Math (including your Cross Math level and best times).
  • Your gems, boosters, block skins and daily-reward streak.
  • Your sound, vibration and language settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not use the internet: all artwork, sounds and puzzles are bundled inside the app or generated on your device, and this privacy policy is included in the app so you can read it without a connection. The Rate and Feedback buttons in Settings open the Play Store or your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Device features

Vibration is used only for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

In-game items

Gems are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the game on boosters, hints, block skins and starting blocks. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Children

Merge Number is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
