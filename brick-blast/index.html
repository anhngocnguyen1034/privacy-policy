Privacy Policy

Brick Blast · com.nnastudio.brick · published by anhnn
Effective date: 3 November 2026

anhnn built Brick Blast as a free block puzzle game. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Brick Blast has no account and no sign-in, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking, and does not connect to the internet. We (anhnn) do not collect any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. There is no registration, no user profile, and no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your best score, recent scores and the level you have reached.
  • Your in-game gems, daily-reward and mission progress.
  • Seasonal event progress and rewards.
  • Your sound and vibration settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The game does not use the internet. All artwork, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app. Links in the game's Settings screen (rate the app, view this policy online) open in your own browser or the Play Store, and sending feedback opens your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

In-game items

Gems are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the game on boosters (hammer, shuffle, Bo's strike, continue). They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with real money: the app has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Brick Blast is a general-audience puzzle game. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, please contact us using the address below.

Security

We do not collect personal data, so there is no personal data of yours that we hold or could lose. Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted online with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.

Contact us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at newtrust.contact@gmail.com.
