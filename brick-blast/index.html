Privacy Policy

Gummy Blast · com.nnastudio.brick · published by anhnn
Effective date: 3 November 2026

anhnn built Gummy Blast as a free block puzzle game supported by ads. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Gummy Blast has no account and no sign-in. We (anhnn) do not collect your name, email or other personal information, and your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob; AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no registration and no user profile, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows video ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example free gems, a free booster or a continue) and occasional full-screen ads between games. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) and app-set ID.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent message). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Where US state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information through the same consent tools.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your best score, recent scores and the level you have reached.
  • Your in-game gems, daily-reward and mission progress, and how many reward videos you watched today.
  • Seasonal event progress and rewards.
  • Your sound and vibration settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The game itself works offline: all artwork, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app. The internet connection is used only to load ads. Links in the Settings screen open in your own browser or the Play Store, and sending feedback opens your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Device features

  • Vibration: used only for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

In-game items

Gems are earned by playing and by watching optional reward videos, and can only be spent inside the game on boosters (hammer, shuffle, Bo's strike, continue). They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Children

Gummy Blast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new effective date.
