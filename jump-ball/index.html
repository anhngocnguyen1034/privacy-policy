Privacy Policy

Jump Ball · com.nnastudio.jumpball · published by anhnn
Effective date: 23 November 2026
Short version: Jump Ball does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in. The game works fully offline, and your progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, does not use an advertising ID, and does not include any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not send data to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file in its own private storage on your device:

  • The level you reached and your best score.
  • Your coins, the balls you have unlocked and the ball you have equipped.
  • Your sound and vibration settings.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for short haptic feedback during play. You can switch it off in the game's Settings.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files.

In-game coins

Coins are earned only by playing and can only be spent inside the game to unlock balls. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Links

The Settings screen can open the game's Google Play page (to rate the game) and this policy. Those pages are provided by Google and GitHub and are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

Jump Ball is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information at all, it does not collect personal information from children. If you have any concern, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new effective date.
