Privacy Policy

Sticker Maker: Photo & Video · com.nnastudio.sticker.maker · published by anhnn
Effective date: 10 October 2026

anhnn built Sticker Maker: Photo & Video as a free application. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: there is no account and no sign-in. The photos and videos you turn into stickers are processed on your device and stay there, unless you yourself choose to share a pack by link. We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase to improve the app. The app shows no advertisements and has no in-app purchases.

Information we collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and there is no registration or user profile. The only information that leaves your device is described in the sections Information sent off your device, Usage statistics and Crash reports below.

Photos and videos you choose

To make a sticker you pick a photo or a video from your gallery, or take a new photo with the camera. The app then cuts out the subject, crops, trims and decorates it. Background removal is done on your device by Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation and a model bundled inside the app; your photos and videos are not uploaded for this.

On Android 14 and newer you can grant access to only the photos and videos you select. The app reads the media you pick only to create stickers, and does not scan, upload or analyse the rest of your gallery.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device so you can come back to your work:

  • The sticker packs you create, including the sticker images, pack names and tray icons.
  • Sticker packs, decorations, backgrounds and templates downloaded from our content server.
  • Your settings, such as the display language and dark mode.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also delete individual packs inside the app, or clear everything from your device's app settings. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this data in your own device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account.

Adding stickers to WhatsApp

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, the app makes that pack available to WhatsApp on your device through a content provider that only WhatsApp is allowed to read. The stickers are transferred directly from this app to WhatsApp on the same device. Once added, they are handled by WhatsApp under the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

Information sent off your device

  • Downloading sticker content. The app downloads ready-made sticker packs, decorations, backgrounds, text bubbles and templates from our content server. These are ordinary downloads; no personal information is attached, but as with any request to any website, the server can see your device's IP address.
  • Sharing a pack by link (only when you ask). If you choose to share a sticker pack by link, the app uploads that one pack file to our sharing server and returns a link for you to send. Anyone who has the link can download the pack. Only share packs you are comfortable sharing. Nothing is uploaded unless you use this option. To have an uploaded pack removed, contact us with the link.
  • Sharing a pack as a file. When you share a pack or an image as a file, Android's standard share sheet opens and you choose which app receives it. The app does not send it anywhere on its own.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback from inside the app, the text you write is sent to us through Google Analytics for Firebase. Please do not include personal information in your feedback.
  • Rating the app. If you choose to rate the app, the rating is handled by Google Play. We receive only what Google Play shows every developer.
  • App configuration. The app fetches settings, such as which features are switched on, from Firebase Remote Config.
  • Install source. The app reads the Google Play Install Referrer once to learn how it was installed (for example from a Play Store listing or a campaign link). This does not identify you.

All requests the app makes to the internet use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Usage statistics

We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase so we can decide what to improve. These are events such as which screen was opened, which button was tapped, whether a sticker was created or exported successfully, and whether a pack was added to WhatsApp.

These statistics never include your photos, videos or sticker images. Google attaches to them a per-installation app instance identifier and general technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, and approximate country derived from the IP address. The app does not collect the Android advertising ID. The app instance identifier is reset if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, we collect diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics. This may include your device's IP address, device model, operating system version, the app's state at the time, the time and date, and the technical trace of the fault. We use it to fix faults, not to identify you.

Advertising

The app shows no advertisements, has no in-app purchases, and does not collect the Android advertising ID. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is included in the app but is not started. If a future version shows ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and where the law requires it you will be asked for consent first.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos (read media images / video, or selected photos only on Android 14+; read storage on Android 12 and older) — to let you pick the photos and videos you turn into stickers.
  • Camera — to take a new photo to turn into a sticker. Used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Internet — to download sticker content, share packs by link when you ask, and send the usage statistics and crash reports described above.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or phone identifiers.

Third-party services

The app uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config
  • Google ML Kit
  • WhatsApp, for packs you choose to add

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your sticker packs and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; we have no copy of them. Packs you share by link are kept on our sharing server so the link keeps working; contact us with the link to have one removed. Usage statistics and crash reports are held by Google on our behalf under Firebase's default retention period, after which they are deleted or aggregated. If you want us to delete the diagnostic and usage data associated with your installation, write to the address below and tell us when and on what device you used the app, and we will delete what we can identify.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the app has no accounts, the only data we can connect to a person is what you send us and the pseudonymous usage and diagnostic data described above. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for the usage and diagnostic data is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, for example by sharing a pack by link, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your stickers are protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device, and everything the app sends over the internet is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we use commercially acceptable means to protect the information we handle.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.
