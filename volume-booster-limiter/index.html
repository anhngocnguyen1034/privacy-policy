Privacy Policy

Volume Boost: EQ & Edge Light · com.nnastudio.volume.booster · NNA Studio
Effective date: October 28, 2026

NNA Studio ("we", "us") built Volume Boost: EQ & Edge Light (the "app") as a free, ad-supported application. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short: the app does not ask you to create an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. It does not record or store audio. We do not run a server of our own. Advertising, analytics and crash reports are provided by Google (details below). Song titles and artist names stay on your device.

1. What the app does on your device

The app boosts the music volume, applies an equalizer and bass boost, draws an audio spectrum, shows edge lighting around the screen, and lets you control music players from a notification. To do this it asks for the following permissions. Everything below is processed on your device only.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Android requires this permission to read the audio level of the sound playing on your phone, which is used to animate the spectrum. The app does not record, save or send any audio, and does not listen to your surroundings.
Notification accessLets the app read which song is playing in your music app (title, artist, play/pause state) and control playback. It is used only for this purpose; the app does not read the content of your other notifications.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)Draws the Edge Lighting effect around the screen edges while you use other apps, only if you turn this on in the Edge Lighting screen. The overlay does not block your taps and does not read what is on the screen.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and foreground serviceShows the music control notification and keeps it working in the background.
Modify audio settings, Vibrate, Wake lockChanges volume and audio effects, gives haptic feedback, and keeps the effects running while music plays.
Internet and network stateUsed for advertising, analytics, crash reports and remote configuration, as described below.

The first time you open the main screen, the app explains why it needs notifications, the microphone and notification access before Android asks you for each of them. Display over other apps is asked only when you turn on edge lighting over other apps. Every permission is optional: you can decline it, or withdraw it at any time in your device settings, and the volume and equalizer controls keep working without it.

2. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The app includes the following third-party services. They may collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with the app and ads.

  • Google AdMob — shows ads and measures ad performance. How AdMob uses data
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens are opened). Firebase privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports (device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash). Firebase privacy
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads configuration, such as a switch that can turn ads on or off. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play services and the Google User Messaging Platform — consent management. Google Privacy Policy

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Advertising and your choices

The app is supported by ads: a banner on the home and theme screens, an ad when the app opens or when you return to it, an occasional full-screen ad after you change a setting, and an optional video ad that you can choose to watch to unlock a theme. Full-screen ads are limited in how often they can appear.

  • In regions such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time from Menu → Ad privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you do not consent, ads are not requested.

4. Song information stays on your device

The title and artist of the song playing are read only to display them in the app and in the music control notification. They are not sent anywhere, are not stored by us, and are not shared with any third party.

5. Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your choices: selected theme and unlocked themes, volume and equalizer presets, edge lighting settings, language, your chosen music player, and, if you pick one, a background image that stays on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it; you can also clear it at any time from Settings → Apps → Volume Boost: EQ & Edge Light → Storage.
