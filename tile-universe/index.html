Privacy Policy

Tile Universe · com.nnastudio.tile.universe · published by anhnn
Effective date: 26 October 2026

anhnn built Tile Universe as a free tile-matching puzzle game. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Tile Universe has no account and no sign-in, and we (anhnn) do not collect your personal information. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure those ads, Google's advertising SDK collects some device information, such as your device's advertising ID, as described below. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There is no registration and no user profile, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show ads:

  • Optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one to get a bonus, for example clearing tray slots, doubling gems or a daily-gift bonus.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between levels.

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Information about how you interact with ads and the app.
  • Diagnostic information, such as performance and crash data related to ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by anhnn, and is encrypted in transit.

Google uses this information as described in its Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Ad content in the app is limited to material suitable for general audiences (maximum rating PG).

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • If you are in one of these regions, you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Ad privacy choices reopens Google's consent form.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings: Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can manage how Google shows you ads at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Watching rewarded ads is always optional. You can play the whole game without choosing to watch them.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your level progress and the stars earned on each level.
  • Your in-game gems, boosters, tile skins and daily-gift streak.
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • Your sound, vibration and display language settings.
  • How many free-gem rewards you have used today.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to load and show ads through Google AdMob. All artwork, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app, and the game itself works without a connection. When you are offline, ads and ad rewards are simply not available.

In-game items

Gems, boosters and tile skins are earned by playing (including by optionally watching rewarded ads) and spent inside the game only. They cannot be bought with real money: the app has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Tile Universe is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the address below.

Third-party content

Some tile artwork is based on Microsoft Fluent Emoji (MIT License). Using this artwork involves no data exchange with Microsoft.

Security

We do not collect personal data ourselves, so there is no personal data of yours that we hold or could lose. Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted online with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.
