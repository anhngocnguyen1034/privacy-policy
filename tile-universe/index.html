Privacy Policy

Tile Universe · com.nnastudio.tile.universe · published by anhnn
Effective date: 15 October 2026

anhnn built Tile Universe as a free tile-matching puzzle game. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Tile Universe does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app works fully offline and your progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, gather or upload your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. There is no registration and no user profile.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no attribution or tracking SDK of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your level progress and the stars earned on each level.
  • Your in-game gems, boosters, tile skins and daily-gift streak.
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • Your sound, vibration and display language settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The app does not request internet access and never connects to any server. All artwork, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app. The app sends no data of any kind to anhnn or to any third party.

In-game items

Gems, boosters and tile skins are earned by playing and spent inside the game only. They cannot be bought with real money: the app has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

  • Vibrate — short haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Tile Universe is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no external links inside the gameplay, and no way for users to communicate with one another through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about the app, please contact us using the address below.

Third-party content

Tile artwork is based on Microsoft Fluent Emoji (MIT License). Using this artwork involves no data exchange with Microsoft.

Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data of yours for us to lose. Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.

Contact us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at newtrust.contact@gmail.com.
