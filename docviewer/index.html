Privacy Policy

DocViewer · com.nnastudio.docviewer · published by NNA Studio
Effective date: 4 November 2026

NNA Studio built DocViewer as a free application for reading and editing PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text documents. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: your documents are opened, edited, converted and saved on your device and are never uploaded by the app. There is no account and no cloud sync. This version of the app shows no ads and does not read your advertising ID. The only network request it makes is to Google Firebase Remote Config, to download a few settings when the app starts. NNA Studio does not sell your data and does not run a server of its own.

Your documents

Everything the app does with a document — reading, searching, annotating, signing, filling forms, editing pages, setting a password, compressing, converting Office files to PDF, turning photos into a PDF and text recognition (OCR) — runs entirely on your device. The app never sends a document, its file name or its contents to NNA Studio or to anyone else, and they are never used to select ads.

Text recognition uses Google's ML Kit recogniser, which is bundled inside the app, so page images are processed on the device and are not sent anywhere. As described in Google's ML Kit terms, ML Kit may send anonymous usage and performance information (such as how long recognition took) to Google; this never includes your images or recognised text.

Information collected by third parties

The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and there is no registration or user profile. One Google service collects information automatically:

  • Firebase Remote Config — when the app starts it downloads a few settings that let us change how the app behaves without publishing an update. To do so Firebase receives an app instance ID and basic technical information about the device, such as the device model, operating system version and language. No document content is sent, and your advertising ID is not used.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

DocViewer shows no ads. The app does not request the Android advertising ID permission, so it cannot read your advertising ID, and nothing you do in the app is used to select or measure ads.

If a future version introduces ads, this page will be updated before that version is published, the Data safety information on Google Play will be updated with it, and — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — you will be asked for consent before any personalised ad is shown.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device, in its private storage:

  • Your reading position and bookmarks for each document, and your reading settings.
  • The list of recently opened and favourite documents, and your sort order.
  • The signature you draw, if you save one, so you can reuse it.
  • Your theme and display language.
  • Temporary working copies of documents, which you can clear from Settings → Clear cache.
  • If the app crashes, a technical crash report written to a local file for diagnosis. It is never sent anywhere.

Android's own backup may include your reading settings, positions, bookmarks and saved signature in your Google account backup if you have backup turned on; the document lists are excluded. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Permissions

  • All files access (Android 11 and later) or storage read/write (Android 10 and earlier) — only to find, list and open the documents on your device and, when you ask, to save, rename, delete or overwrite a file. You can refuse it and still open documents one at a time through the system file picker.
  • Internet and network state — used only for Firebase Remote Config, as described above. No document ever leaves your device over the network.

Photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker, and photos are taken with your own camera app, so the app needs no photo or camera permission. It requests no other permissions.

Sharing

A document or image leaves the app only when you tap Share, Print or Save and choose the destination yourself through Android's standard screens.

Children's privacy

DocViewer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. There are no in-app purchases and no way for users to communicate with one another through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, please contact us using the address below.

Security

Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox. The settings the app downloads from Firebase travel over encrypted connections, and the information Firebase receives is handled under Google's security practices. Password-protected PDFs you create are encrypted with AES-256.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.
