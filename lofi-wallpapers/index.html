Privacy Policy

Lofi Wallpapers – Anime Aesthetic (com.nnastudio.wallpaper) · NNA Studio
Effective date: November 2, 2026
In short: you do not need an account. Your favorites, downloads and the photos or videos you choose stay on your device, and we do not run servers of our own. Wallpapers are downloaded from GitHub. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, sells an optional Premium subscription through Google Play, and uses Google Firebase for pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Favorites, downloaded wallpapers and settings (schedule, daily rotation, live wallpaper options, language) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Your own photos and videos. When you use "Your photo" or "Use your own video", you pick a file in Android's system picker. The app only receives the file you picked, copies it into its private storage to set it as your wallpaper, and never uploads it. The app does not request permission to read your photo library.
  • Unlocked premium wallpapers. When you watch a rewarded ad to unlock a wallpaper for 24 hours, the unlock time is stored on your device only.

3. Wallpaper downloads

The wallpaper catalog and images are downloaded from a public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information when the app downloads a file. We do not receive this information. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects pseudonymous usage information such as screens opened, features used, app version, device model, OS version, language, country and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects technical crash information (stack trace, device state, app version) when the app crashes.
  • If you send feedback from the app, the text you type is sent as part of the usage statistics so we can read it. Please do not include personal information in it.

This information does not include your name, contacts, or the photos and videos you pick. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

If you do not have Premium, the app shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • an occasional full-screen ad when you come back to the app after a while, or after you finish setting or downloading a wallpaper (never right after another ad);
  • native ads between wallpapers in some lists, clearly labeled "Ad", and banner ads at the bottom of some screens;
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to unlock a premium wallpaper for 24 hours.

To select and measure ads, Google receives your device's advertising ID together with technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only receives the purchase status needed to turn Premium on, which also removes all ads. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

7. Your rights and deleting data

  • We do not hold an account or profile about you. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • You may ask for access to, or deletion of, the statistics and crash data associated with your installation, or object to its processing (for example under the GDPR or CCPA). Write to the address below and tell us roughly when and on which device you used the app. We do not sell personal information.
  • Statistics and crash reports are kept by Google on our behalf under Firebase's default retention periods, after which they are deleted or aggregated.

Legal basis (EEA/UK users). We rely on your consent for ads that use the advertising ID (you can withdraw it under Settings → Privacy options), and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it for crash reports and usage statistics.

International transfers. Google and GitHub process information on servers in several countries, including the United States. Where required, they rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Content

The wallpapers in the app are original works created for this app (AI-generated images and animations made from them). They do not depict real people.

9. Security

Data created by the app is protected by Android's per-app sandbox, and information sent to Google is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Links to other sites

Ads and this policy may link to third-party sites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date and included in later versions of the app.
