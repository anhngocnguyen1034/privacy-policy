Privacy Policy

ClearSound: Speaker & Storage · com.nnastudio.clean.speaker · published by anhnn
Effective date: October 20, 2026

ClearSound: Speaker & Storage (the "app") is a free utility that cleans your phone speaker and helps you find junk, large files, duplicate photos and unused apps. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short:

  • No account, sign-in or registration. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • Your files, photos and app list are analysed only on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases. The app does not use the advertising ID: the AD_ID permission is removed.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, microphone recordings or camera images. There is no user profile.

Files, photos and apps on your device

To clean your phone, the app reads information about media and apps on your device. All of this is processed locally and is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Photos, videos and audio (via Android's media library): to find junk, large files, duplicate or similar photos and to show how much space each category uses.
  • Installed apps and usage access: to list apps, show their storage and battery/usage impact, and let you uninstall them. Usage access is optional and granted by you in Android settings.
  • Storage and device information (free space, memory, battery, system details): to display the System Info and storage screens.

Files are deleted only after you confirm. For media belonging to other apps, Android shows its own system confirmation dialog. The app does not have the "all files access" permission and can only see media files that Android's media library exposes to it.

Speaker cleaning

Speaker cleaning plays test tones and sound waves through your device speaker, and may adjust audio settings while it runs. Nothing is recorded.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device so it can work and remember your choices:

  • Your settings, such as display language and sound/cleaning presets and options.
  • Whether you have completed the welcome screens.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings. Files you have not deleted stay on your device.

Usage statistics and crash reports

The app uses two Google services to understand how it is used and to fix problems:

  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous events such as which screens are opened and which features are used. Events do not include the contents or names of your files, photos or apps.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports with technical details (device model, Android version, app version, stack trace).

These services identify the app installation with a random Firebase installation ID and may receive the device's IP address, approximate region, general device information and how the app was installed. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising. It is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and kept only as long as Google's retention settings for these services allow.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. Every cleaning feature, and this Privacy Policy page inside the app, works without an internet connection.

Feedback and ratings

When you send feedback from Settings, the app opens your own email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself, and we use your email address and message only to reply to you. Rating the app opens its page on Google Play.

Permissions

  • Read photos, videos and audio (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED; on Android 12 and older READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): to scan media for junk, large files and duplicate photos, and to delete the files you choose.
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): optional, granted by you in Android settings, to show how much each app is used.
  • Request to delete packages (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES): to open Android's uninstall dialog for apps you choose. Android always asks you to confirm.
  • Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to play the speaker-cleaning tones.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to send the statistics described above.

Google Firebase also adds WAKE_LOCK (to finish sending statistics) and the Google Play install referrer permission (to tell which store the app was installed from). The app requests no other permissions, and does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or "all files access".

Sharing of information

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named on this page (Google Firebase), which processes it only to provide its services.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

This section applies to ClearSound: Speaker & Storage (com.nnastudio.clean.speaker), published by anhnn. The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

  • Data on your device (settings and cleaning presets): uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (held by Google Firebase): email newtrust.contact@gmail.com with the subject “ClearSound — data deletion request”. We will reply within 30 days and delete the data we can associate with your request. Because this data is not linked to your identity, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage also resets the random Firebase installation ID, so new data cannot be connected to old data.

We do not hold your files, photos or app list, so there is nothing else to delete.

Security

Data stored by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device. Information sent to Firebase is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact us at newtrust.contact@gmail.com, or use the Feedback option in the app's Settings screen.
