Privacy Policy

CubeMind · com.nnastudio.cube · published by NNA Studio
Effective date: 19 November 2026

NNA Studio built CubeMind as a free application that solves puzzle cubes (3x3, 2x2, 4x4, 5x5, Pyraminx, Megaminx and more) and teaches you the moves. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: the cube colours you enter are solved on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account and no cloud sync. The app uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config) and may show ads from Google AdMob. NNA Studio does not sell your data and does not run a server of its own.

Your cube

Entering or scanning colours, checking the cube for mistakes, computing the solution and playing it back all run entirely on your device, with or without an internet connection. The colours, the camera images and the solutions are never sent to NNA Studio or to anyone else.

Information collected by third parties

The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location, and there is no registration or user profile. Google services collect some information automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage events (for example which screens are opened and whether a solve succeeded) and basic technical information such as the device model, operating system version, language and an app instance ID. It never includes the colours of your cube.
  • Firebase Remote Config — when the app starts it downloads a few settings, such as whether ads are turned on, so we can change how the app behaves without publishing an update.
  • Google AdMob — when ads are turned on, AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads you see and tap, to show and measure ads.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and consent

CubeMind may show banner, interstitial, native and app open ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device, in its private storage:

  • The solver's lookup tables, computed on the first launch (about 7 MB), so later solves are instant.
  • Your display language and preferences, including the algorithms you picked in the lessons.
  • Your timer history (times, penalties and scrambles) and the days you practised, used for the statistics and the streak. You can delete the times from the timer screen.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — used only by Firebase and AdMob as described above. Solving never needs the network.
  • Vibrate — for a short vibration on each move of the solution player.
  • Camera (optional, asked when you tap Scan with camera) — to read the colours of your cube. The live camera picture is analysed on your device, frame by frame, and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. You can refuse it and enter the colours by hand.

Children's privacy

CubeMind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, please contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.
