Privacy Policy

Plank Pop: Wood Screw Puzzle · com.nnastudio.woodpuzzel · published by anhnn
Effective date: 27 November 2026

anhnn built Plank Pop as a free wood screw puzzle game supported by ads. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Plank Pop has no account and no sign-in. We (anhnn) do not collect your name, email or any other personal information, and your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase to count how far players get and to receive crash reports. Both are described below, and you can switch the Firebase part off inside the game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no registration and no user profile. We run no servers of our own: the only information that leaves your device goes to Google, for the two purposes described in the next two sections.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows video ads you choose to watch for a reward (extra time, or one extra hole, after you lose a board) and occasional full-screen ads in the break between levels. There is always a way to carry on without watching. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) and app-set ID.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's advertising policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

So we can tell which levels are too hard and which bugs are hurting real players, the app sends Google Firebase a small number of events: a level starting, a level being won or lost (with the number of stars, how long it took and whether time ran out or the board got stuck), which screen you opened, when a booster or an in-game coin is spent, when an ad is shown, and when the tutorial is finished. It also sends the language the app is set to, and an automatic crash report if the game stops working.

These events describe the game, not you. We never send your name, email, contacts, location or the contents of your saved game. Firebase does use a device identifier to group events from the same installation.

You can turn all of this off: Settings → Privacy Policy → Share usage data. With it off, nothing is sent — neither events nor crash reports — and the game remembers that choice.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent message). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Policy → Ad privacy options.
  • You can switch off analytics and crash reporting in Settings → Privacy Policy → Share usage data.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Where US state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information through the same consent tools.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • The levels you have reached and the stars you earned in each game mode.
  • Your in-game coins, diamonds, boosters and reward-chest progress.
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • Your settings: sound, language, chosen background, and whether usage data is shared.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings, or with Settings → Reset Progress inside the game.

Internet access

The puzzle itself works offline: all artwork, sounds and levels are bundled inside the app. An internet connection is used only to load ads and to send the events described above. Links in the Settings screen open in your own browser or the Play Store, and sending feedback opens your own email app — nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Permissions

The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage. It declares two things: vibration, used only for short haptic feedback during play (you can turn sound and vibration off in Settings), and access to the advertising ID, which Google AdMob needs as described above.

In-game items

Coins, diamonds and boosters (screwdriver, drill, hammer, freeze, lightning) are earned by playing and from reward chests, and can only be used inside the game. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Children

Plank Pop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if the advertising or analytics described above change), the new version will be published at this address before that version is released, with a new effective date.
