Privacy Policy

Keyboard Effects · com.nnastudio.keyboard · NNA Studio
Effective date: October 29, 2026

NNA Studio ("we", "us") built Keyboard Effects (the "app") as a free application. The app is a keyboard (an Android input method) with themes, fonts, key sounds and effects. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short: what you type stays on your device. The keyboard does not record, store, upload or share the text you type, and we do not run a server of our own. The app shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and does not ask you to create an account. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are provided by Google Firebase (details below); they never contain what you type.

1. What you type

When you enable any third-party keyboard, Android shows a standard warning that the keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers". Android shows this for every keyboard. Here is what Keyboard Effects actually does:

  • Your keystrokes are turned into text on your device and passed directly to the app you are typing in. They are not logged, not saved, and not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Vietnamese typing (Telex / VNI) reads the few characters just before the cursor, in memory only, to place tone marks correctly. It is switched off automatically in password, email and web address fields.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (section 4) never include the text you type, your clipboard, or what you say to voice input.

3. Data stored on your device

The following are saved only in the app's private storage on your device, and never leave it:

  • Your keyboard settings: theme, font style, key sound, key effect, key height, vibration, Vietnamese typing method, and app language.
  • Keyboards you design yourself, including any background photo you choose (a cropped copy is kept inside the app).
  • Your favorite themes, recently used emoji, and emoji texts you create.
  • Clipboard history — off by default. If you turn it on in the keyboard's clipboard panel, the text you copy while it is on is kept so you can paste it again. Items you do not pin are deleted automatically after one hour, and at most 50 are kept; pinned items stay until you delete them. If you copy something sensitive, such as a password, it is saved there too, so delete it from the clipboard panel or turn the feature off. It is never uploaded.

You can delete these from inside the app (for example the delete option on the Personal screen and in the clipboard panel). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Keyboard Effects → Storage, deletes all of them. We have no copy.

4. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The app includes the following Google services. They may collect information such as an app-instance identifier, the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and which screens of the app are opened.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics, such as app opens and which screens are viewed. Firebase privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the app's state at the time of the crash, and the stack trace of the fault). Firebase privacy
  • Google Play services, including Google Play's in-app review if you choose to rate the app. Google Privacy Policy
  • The speech recognition service on your device, usually Google's, for voice typing (section 2).

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
