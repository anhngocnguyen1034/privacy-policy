Privacy Policy

Keyboard Effects · com.nnastudio.keyboard · NNA Studio
Effective date: October 29, 2026 (ads updated)

NNA Studio ("we", "us") built Keyboard Effects (the "app") as a free application. The app is a keyboard (an Android input method) with themes, fonts, key sounds and effects. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short: what you type stays on your device. The keyboard does not record, store, upload or share the text you type, and we do not run a server of our own. The app has no in-app purchases and does not ask you to create an account. It shows ads from Google AdMob only inside the app, never on the keyboard while you type: optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch to unlock premium items, and an occasional full-screen ad after you apply a theme or save a keyboard. Pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports are provided by Google Firebase. None of these ever contain what you type (details below).

1. What you type

When you enable any third-party keyboard, Android shows a standard warning that the keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers". Android shows this for every keyboard. Here is what Keyboard Effects actually does:

  • Your keystrokes are turned into text on your device and passed directly to the app you are typing in. They are not logged, not saved, and not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Vietnamese typing (Telex / VNI) reads the few characters just before the cursor, in memory only, to place tone marks correctly. It is switched off automatically in password, email and web address fields.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and ad requests (sections 4 and 5) never include the text you type, your clipboard, or what you say to voice input.

3. Data stored on your device

The following are saved only in the app's private storage on your device, and never leave it:

  • Your keyboard settings: theme, font style, key sound, key effect, key height, vibration, Vietnamese typing method, and app language.
  • Keyboards you design yourself, including any background photo you choose (a cropped copy is kept inside the app).
  • Your favorite themes, the premium items you have unlocked, recently used emoji, and emoji texts you create.
  • Clipboard history — off by default. If you turn it on in the keyboard's clipboard panel, the text you copy while it is on is kept so you can paste it again. Items you do not pin are deleted automatically after one hour, and at most 50 are kept; pinned items stay until you delete them. If you copy something sensitive, such as a password, it is saved there too, so delete it from the clipboard panel or turn the feature off. It is never uploaded.

You can delete these from inside the app (for example the delete option on the Personal screen and in the clipboard panel). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Keyboard Effects → Storage, deletes all of them. We have no copy.

4. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The app includes the following Google services. They may collect information such as an app-instance identifier, the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and which screens of the app are opened.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — pseudonymous usage statistics (tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name), such as app opens and which screens are viewed. Firebase privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the app's state at the time of the crash, and the stack trace of the fault). Firebase privacy
  • Google Play services, including Google Play's in-app review if you choose to rate the app. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform — ads and the ad consent form, described in section 5. How AdMob uses data
  • The speech recognition service on your device, usually Google's, for voice typing (section 2).

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Ads and your choices

The app shows two kinds of ads from Google AdMob, only inside the app:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch. Some premium themes, fonts, typing effects and key sounds are marked with a lock. When you tap one, the app asks whether you want to watch a short video ad to unlock it; after you watch it, that item is unlocked permanently on your device. Nothing plays unless you agree. You can also unlock every premium theme at once by watching five of these ads, one at a time whenever you like; the count is kept on your device.
  • An occasional full-screen ad after you apply a theme or save a keyboard you designed, once the task is done. It is shown at most every second time, never right after another ad, and never more than once a minute.

There are no banner ads, no ads when the app opens or closes, and no ads on the keyboard while you type.

To select and measure the ad, Google receives your device's advertising ID together with technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and whether the ad was shown or tapped. Nothing you type is ever sent to Google AdMob or to any advertiser.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options. If ads cannot be shown because consent was not given, locked items are unlocked without an ad.
  • On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
