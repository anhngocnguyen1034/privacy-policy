Privacy Policy

Jigsaw Nest: Art Puzzle · com.nnastudio.jigsawnest · published by newtrust
Effective date: 23 September 2026

newtrust built Jigsaw Nest: Art Puzzle as a free application. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Jigsaw Nest has no account, no sign-in and no advertising. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos. To keep the app stable and improve it, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

The app does not ask for, gather or upload your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other information that identifies you personally. There is no registration and no user profile.

The app uses two services from Google Firebase, which collect the following information automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics such as which screens are opened, puzzles started and completed, session length, app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country or region (derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier. We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — when the app crashes or hits a serious error: the crash report (technical details of the error), the time it happened, app version, device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

This information is not linked to your name or any account, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to build advertising profiles. The app does not use your device's advertising ID. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically.

The app contains no advertising SDK and shows no advertisements.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Puzzles in progress, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Your best scores, times and move counts for completed puzzles.
  • Your in-game points, the puzzles you have unlocked with them, and your favorite puzzles.
  • Your daily reward check-in days.
  • Your preferences: theme, board background, sound, vibration, multi-select and display language.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

If you have turned on Android's own backup feature, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; newtrust has no access to it.

Internet access

All puzzle artwork is bundled inside the app itself, and the game works fully offline. The app uses the internet only to send the anonymous analytics and crash information described above to Google Firebase. Your game progress and preferences are never uploaded.

In-game points and purchases

Points are earned only by playing and are used only to unlock puzzles inside the app. They have no monetary value. The app has no in-app purchases and no real-money payments.

Permissions

  • Internet and Network state — used only by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to send the information described above.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Jigsaw Nest is a general-audience puzzle game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics and crash information described above is anonymous and is not used for advertising. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no external links inside the gameplay and no way for users to communicate with one another through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about the app, please contact us using the address below.

Security

Analytics and crash information is sent to Google Firebase over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data stored by the app on your device is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the app. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data associated with your installation, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.
