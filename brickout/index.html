Privacy Policy

Prism Breaker · com.nnastudio.brickout · published by anhnn
Effective date: 16 November 2026

anhnn built Prism Breaker (the "Game") as a free brick-breaker puzzle game. This page explains what the Game does and does not do with information. By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: there is no account of our own and we run no servers. Your progress is saved only on your device. The Game may show ads served by Google AdMob and may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Google.

Data stored on your device

The Game keeps the following on your device so you can continue playing:

  • Game progress: stars for each level, best Endless score, daily-challenge result, gems, and the ball skins and brick themes you own and use.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration and display language.

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include it in your own device backup, managed by Google under your Google account.

Advertising

The Game may be supported by ads from Google AdMob: rewarded video ads that play only after you choose to watch one (for example for a free revive or bonus gems), and full-screen ads shown between games. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required) are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and can change that choice later from the Game's Settings screen.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional purchases (such as removing ads or gem packs). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment card or billing details. We receive only a confirmation of what was bought so the Game can deliver it. Gems earned or bought are used only inside the Game and have no real-world value.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games Services, Google receives the achievements you unlock and the scores you submit to leaderboards, and links them to your Play Games profile (player name and avatar). This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your email address or other account details. Signing in is optional; the Game works without it.

Feedback

If you tap Feedback in Settings, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. It includes the Game version, device model, Android version and language so we can investigate problems; you can read and edit it before sending. We receive it only if you send it, and use it only to reply and to fix issues.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — to load ads, show the consent form, process purchases and use Google Play Games.
  • Vibrate — for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob as described above.

The Game does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Third-party services

The Game uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services
  • Google AdMob

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game; we have no copy of it. Data held by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games) can be managed or deleted through your Google Account and Play Games settings. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request; ask us and we will delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the Game has no accounts of its own, the only data we can connect to a person is what you send us. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete what we can identify.

Security

Your progress is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox, and the third-party services above encrypt data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use commercially acceptable means to protect the information we handle.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at newtrust.contact@gmail.com, or use the Feedback option in the Game's Settings screen.
