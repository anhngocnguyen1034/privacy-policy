Privacy Policy

Cat Connect · com.nnastudio.catconnect · published by anhnn
Effective date: 26 November 2026
Short version: Cat Connect does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no sign-in. The game works fully offline, and your progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, does not use an advertising ID, and does not include any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not send data to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file in its own private storage on your device: your best score, the highest round you reached, your coins, the ball sets you unlocked and the one in use, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your vibration setting. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

Permissions

Vibration: for short haptic feedback during play (you can switch it off in Settings). The game does not request access to the Internet, your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos or files.

In-game coins

Coins are earned only by playing and can only be spent inside the game to unlock ball sets. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Feedback by email

When you tap Feedback in Settings, your email app opens a draft addressed to us. The draft is pre-filled with the game version, your device model and operating system version so we can help; you can edit or delete that before sending. If you send the email, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, use them only to reply to you, and do not share them with anyone.

Links

Settings can open the game's Google Play page (to rate the game) and the online copy of this policy. Those pages are run by Google and GitHub under their own privacy policies.

Children

Cat Connect is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information at all, it does not collect personal information from children. If you have any concern, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the game, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: newtrust.contact@gmail.com

Dữ liệu lưu trên thiết bị

Để ghi nhớ tiến trình, game lưu một tệp nhỏ trong vùng lưu trữ riêng của game trên thiết bị: điểm kỷ lục, vòng cao nhất bạn đạt được, số xu, các bộ bóng đã mở khóa và bộ đang dùng, việc bạn đã xem hướng dẫn hay chưa, và cài đặt rung. Dữ liệu này không bao giờ rời khỏi thiết bị và bị xóa khi bạn gỡ game hoặc xóa dữ liệu của game trong cài đặt thiết bị.

Quyền truy cập

Xu chỉ kiếm được bằng cách chơi và chỉ dùng trong game để mở khóa các bộ bóng. Xu không có giá trị ngoài đời thực và không thể mua bằng tiền.

Khi bạn chạm Góp ý trong Cài đặt, ứng dụng email của bạn mở một thư nháp gửi tới chúng tôi.
