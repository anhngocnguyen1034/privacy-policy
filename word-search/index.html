Privacy Policy

Word Search · com.nnastudio.wordsearch · published by anhnn
Effective date: 28 November 2026

anhnn built Word Search as a free word puzzle game. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Word Search has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We (anhnn) do not collect any information about you, and your game progress stays on your device. The game uses the internet only to show this policy and, if you choose, to let you rate it on Google Play.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, gather or receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any device identifier. There is no registration and no user profile, and we run no servers. The app does not include any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • The level you have reached on the journey, and an unfinished puzzle so you can continue it later.
  • Your in-game coins and bonus-word meter.
  • Your Daily Challenge streak and best streak.
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • Your settings: sound and vibration.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings (Settings → Apps → Word Search → Storage → Clear data).

Device backup

If you have turned on backup for your Android device, Android may include the game's progress in your device backup, so it can be restored when you set up a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and your Google account under your own backup settings; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your device settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

Internet access

The game works fully offline. All puzzles, word lists, sounds and graphics are bundled inside the app, and the Daily Challenge puzzle is created on your device from the date. The internet is used only in these cases, all started by you or by Google Play:

  • Settings → Privacy Policy loads this page inside the app. Like any web page, it is served by GitHub Pages, which receives the usual technical request data (such as your IP address) needed to deliver it.
  • Rating the game. Once, after you have finished the first two regions, and whenever you tap Settings → Rate the game and choose a high rating, the game asks Google Play to show its in-app review card, or opens the game's Play Store page. Google Play handles your rating and review under Google's privacy policy; we only see the public review you choose to post.
  • Sending feedback. Settings → Send feedback, or a low rating, opens your own email app with a message to us already written. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

Permissions

The app declares two permissions: vibration, used only for short haptic feedback while you swipe across letters and when you find a word (you can turn vibration and sound off in Settings), and internet access, used only as described above. The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

In-game items

Coins are earned by playing (finishing levels, the Daily Challenge and bonus words) and can only be used inside the game for hints. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought with money.

Children

Word Search is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that use data differently (for example advertising or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated at this address before that version is released, with a new effective date.
