Privacy Policy

Emoji Merge · com.anhnn.emoji_merge · published by anhnn
Effective date: 22 October 2026

anhnn built Emoji Merge as a free application. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Emoji Merge has no account and no sign-in, and everything you create stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These Google services collect some device information, described below. anhnn does not sell your data.

Information we collect

The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or photos, and there is no registration or user profile. The images you create are not uploaded anywhere.

The app does include the following third-party services from Google, which collect information automatically when you use the app:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to show ads based on your interests.
  • Firebase Analytics — app usage events such as which screens are opened, emoji merges, saves and mini-game levels, together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system version and approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports containing the technical state of the app and device at the moment of a crash, so we can fix bugs.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and your choices

Emoji Merge is free and is supported by ads: a banner on the home screen, an occasional full-screen ad between screens, an ad when the app starts, and an optional video ad you may choose to watch to skip a mini-game level. Full-screen ads are limited in how often they can appear.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On any Android device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data never leaves your device:

  • The emoji combinations you choose to save, kept in the app's own cache folder.
  • Your mini-game level progress and your sound on/off preference.
  • The display language you select in Settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The app uses internet access to download merged emoji artwork from Google's public content delivery network at gstatic.com, and for the advertising, analytics and crash-reporting services listed above. The artwork downloads are ordinary image requests with no information about you attached, although, as with any request to any website, the server receiving it can see your device's IP address. All other artwork is bundled inside the app itself. anhnn does not run a server of its own.

Saving and sharing images

When you tap Save, the app writes the resulting image to your device's picture gallery. On Android 9 and older this uses the storage permission declared in the app; on Android 10 and newer no permission is required. When you tap Share, Android's standard share sheet opens and you choose which app receives the image. The app does not upload images anywhere on its own and has no server of its own.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to download merged emoji artwork and to load ads, as described above.
  • Advertising ID — read by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save an image you asked to save into your gallery.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Emoji Merge is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. There are no in-app purchases and no way for users to communicate with one another through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, please contact us using the address below.

Security

Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device. Information collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is sent over encrypted connections and handled under Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.

Contact us
