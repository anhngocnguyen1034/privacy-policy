Privacy Policy

Emoji Merge · com.anhnn.emoji_merge · published by anhnn
Effective date: 5 September 2026

anhnn built Emoji Merge as a free application. This page explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Short version: Emoji Merge does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, gather or upload your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. There is no registration and no user profile.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no attribution or tracking SDK of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your progress. This data never leaves your device:

  • The emoji combinations you choose to save, kept in the app's own cache folder.
  • Your mini-game level progress and your sound on/off preference.
  • The display language you select in Settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

Internet access

The app requests internet access for one purpose only: to download merged emoji artwork from Google's public content delivery network at gstatic.com. These are ordinary image downloads. No information about you is attached to them, but as with any request to any website, the server receiving it can see your device's IP address. Google's handling of such requests is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

All other artwork used by the app is bundled inside the app itself and requires no network access. The app sends no data of any kind to anhnn or to any third party.

Saving and sharing images

When you tap Save, the app writes the resulting image to your device's picture gallery. On Android 9 and older this uses the storage permission declared in the app; on Android 10 and newer no permission is required. When you tap Share, Android's standard share sheet opens and you choose which app receives the image. The app does not upload images anywhere on its own and has no server of its own.

Permissions

  • Internet — to download merged emoji artwork, as described above.
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save an image you asked to save into your gallery.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Emoji Merge is a general-audience entertainment app suitable for all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no external links inside the gameplay, and no way for users to communicate with one another through the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about the app, please contact us using the address below.

Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data of yours for us to lose. Data created by the app is protected by Android's standard per-app sandbox on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and will be included in a subsequent version of the app.

Contact us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at newtrust.contact@gmail.com, or use the Feedback option in the app's Settings screen.
